# REVIEW.md

Review material issues only.

## Block merge to ai-lab for
- broken existing navigation or language controls;
- missing local assets or console-breaking JavaScript errors;
- product facts/claims presented without evidence;
- concept visuals presented as real product photography;
- fake checkout/cart/order behavior;
- secrets or personal data exposure;
- major mobile/accessibility regressions;
- unnecessary dependencies or external services;
- unrelated changes that make rollback difficult.

## Do not block for
- subjective typography taste;
- harmless naming/style preferences;
- experimental visual differences when they are intentional and reversible.

A finding should state the file/location, evidence, impact, and smallest safe fix.
